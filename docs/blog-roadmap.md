# Training Labs blog roadmap

TL;DR: two posts a day, published at 6 PM IST by the `training-labs-daily-blogs` routine. Each
run takes the next two `todo` rows, in order, writes them per `docs/blog-playbook.md`, and marks
them `done` here in the same PR.

## How to use this file

- Take the first two `todo` rows. Pairs are ordered so each day mixes one core sales training or
  pricing topic with one vertical (healthcare, education, insurance, BPO, hospitality...).
- Skip a row only if a live search shows it cannot be won or a sister site already targets the
  same primary keyword. Mark it `skipped (<reason>)` and take the next row.
- On publish, change the status to `done YYYY-MM-DD /blog/<slug>`.
- Append new rows at the bottom when rival research or sales calls surface a better topic.
  Keep the evidence column honest: name the source.

## Sister sites: do not target their keywords

Same owner, so these sites must not compete for one search. Link to them instead.

- snapsdr.com: sales role play scenarios, AI sales coaching tools, real-time sales coaching,
  sales call scorecard, sales ramp time, call center simulation software, AI cold calling,
  AI SDR/BDR, demo scripts and follow-ups, Hyperbound/Vapi/Karumi/Storylane alternatives.
- toughtongueai.com: customer service role play scenarios, AI roleplay tools for corporate
  training, AI negotiation training tools, AI mock interview platforms, admissions call script,
  practicing difficult conversations with AI, AI oral exams, cold calling anxiety.

Check both blog indexes each run; they publish daily.
